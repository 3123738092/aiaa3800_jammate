# JamMate

## Register

product

## Users

Ordinary listeners and students without workstation experience, exploring a jam by speaking, humming or clapping. Keyboard play is optional. Developers inspect the same real session through a separate Inspector.

## Product Purpose

Start and shape a band without navigating model configuration. Keep existing backend contracts and real input/output evidence accessible.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, playful, quiet. References supplied by the user: Portable CRT's physical controls, precise music hardware, spatial music browsers and Macintosh material coherence.

## Anti-references

Repeated dashboard cards, excessive entry points, decorative glass, gratuitous gradients, simulated model traces and generic AI product headings.

## Design Principles

- Point to a familiar musical object, then speak or type; no workstation knowledge required.
- One primary input with explicit speech, humming and rhythm modes.
- Familiar objects carry targeting; the detailed hardware keyboard remains an optional play surface.
- Lighting and depth belong to a coherent physical scene.
- Motion represents actual playback or direct interaction.
- Settings, capture review and real diagnostics must receive the same visual care as the main scene.
- No slogans, invented logo marks or decorative directional arrows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard operable controls, visible focus, descriptive labels, reduced motion support and responsive layouts as baseline implementation defaults. No special accessibility requirements were supplied.
